Pilot results

Descriptive estimates are reported with exact tests and internal resampling intervals. No audience effect or causal claim is made.

HypothesisEstimateExact testInterval
H1 · accountability cueAI 0.400 vs comparison 0.250 · RD 0.150Fisher p = 0.431[-0.15, 0.55]
H2 · named actorAI 1.000 vs comparison 0.900 · RD 0.100Fisher p = 0.540[0, 0.25]
Exploratory · uncertaintyAI 0.800 vs comparison 0.200 · RD 0.600Fisher p = 0.004[0.25, 0.90]

H1 and H2 do not clear conventional significance thresholds. The exploratory uncertainty result is descriptive and should be treated as hypothesis-generating.

Claim boundaries
